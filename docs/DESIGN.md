# Product Design Contract — Noveno Website

Visual and interaction source of truth shared by design, implementation, browser QA, and review. This file records the **accepted visual direction** (locked by founder decision 2026-08-14, second review: the flowchart/route language is rejected; **third review 2026-09: contextual photography is retired from the public visual language** — see §3.5; **fourth review 2026-10: the homepage hero becomes brand artwork and Insights becomes وبلاگ** — see §3.6 and the decision log §16). The thesis, tokens, composition, media, and proof plan below are the contract; later build/review work must implement this direction, not invent a new one. Changes require a deliberate, documented decision (§16).

## 0. Owner direction (founder brief — recorded verbatim in intent, 2026-10)

These are **owner decisions**, not agent proposals. Everything not listed here keeps the previously accepted direction (§2–§14 stay in force, including the locked palette, type system, themes, logo, and the rejected flowchart grammar).

- **Positioning:** a small, founder-led specialist that builds **measurable lead-generation systems for Iranian service businesses** — not a broad full-service agency.
- **Thesis line:** «سایت شما باید مشتری واقعی بیاورد، نه فقط بازدید.» — the first viewport must answer, in seconds: what Noveno does, who it is for, what business outcome it serves, what to do next.
- **Visual/product direction:** minimal, premium, modern, confident; Persian RTL; **keep the existing Noveno brand palette unless a concrete usability/accessibility problem is measured**; more whitespace and stronger hierarchy; fewer simultaneous messages; short sections instead of explanatory walls; strong typography and concise copy; one primary action per section where practical.
- **Mobile is first-class**, not a shrunk desktop.
- **Explicitly rejected:** generic SaaS-template aesthetics, card walls, unnecessary badges, fake dashboards, decorative metrics, meaningless gradients/effects, and AI-generated marketing verbosity. Sections that repeat an explanation already given elsewhere are removed rather than restated.
- **Proof honesty (non-negotiable):** no demo/concept/illustrative item may present expected outcomes as measured results. Real measured results are published only when the repository holds a defensible source. Credibility outranks impressive numbers.
- **Pricing:** public prices in **تومان** with clear «از …» starting prices — three primary offers; no live USD/FX widget and no internal USD model exposed to customers.
- **Founder visibility:** the founder must be visible enough that Noveno reads as a real specialist business. No invented experience, client counts, credentials, team size, or awards. No generated or substituted portrait.
- **Contact identity:** prefer a verified domain mailbox only if one is actually configured; otherwise keep the working address and report the gap. Never replace a working address with an invented one.
- **Initial contact:** the first form asks only what is needed to qualify and contact a lead. It must never show false success, and a failure must be actionable.

_Agent-labelled proposals (reversible, not owner decisions):_ the «قیمت» page lives at `/pricing` (new route, no existing URL changed); recurring support is presented as three productized monthly options; e-commerce is demoted to a secondary custom-project line; the primary CTA keeps its established label «درخواست بررسی مسیر جذب» because changing it would break accepted product language; no dedicated `/industries/*` routes are created until a niche is actually proven.

- **Eighth review (2026-10, owner): وبلاگ returns to the header and to the homepage.** The owner asked, in intent, to «add blog to the header of website. add blog section to the home page». Two changes, both explicit owner choices:
  1. **Primary navigation returns to six links:** «خانه · راهکارها · نمونه‌کار · **وبلاگ** · قیمت · درباره» plus the single primary action. `/blog` keeps its URL, its SEO ownership and its canonical routes; the mobile menu and the footer list it as well. The fifth review's condition for this move is now met: the section holds real published writing (six articles), not placeholders.
  2. **The homepage gets one blog section back**, placed after the proof section and before the offers: «what we do → what we have built → what we write about it → what it costs». Everything the fifth review removed for message discipline stays removed; only this one section is restored, and it is kept short (§3.8).

  _Agent-labelled proposal (reversible, not an owner decision):_ the section's composition, copy and placement inside the sequence are implementation choices recorded in §3.8; the owner decision is only that the blog belongs in the header and on the homepage.

- **Seventh review (2026-10, owner): build the improvement pass.** The owner accepted the evidence-first improvement pass recorded in `docs/exec-plans/active/ui-ux-improvement-pass.md` ("انجام بده"). Scope is a quality pass **inside** this contract: the locked visual direction, palette, type system, themes, logo, and the rejected flowchart grammar all stay in force, and no new owner style or color choice was supplied. Four things are in scope: FAQ answers must be readable when JavaScript does not run; the `/work` selected-filter chip must pass 4.5:1 on `primary-soft`; the offer/price surface must stop rendering three identical copies; and the §3.6 signature artwork must execute its own thesis legibly at 1440 and 390. No new section, route, dependency, price, or conversion claim.

## 1. Experience brief

- Product / surface: Noveno (نوونو) marketing website — Persian-first, RTL-first.
- Primary audience: owners/decision-makers of small and medium Iranian service businesses, non-technical, mobile-first, often on slow/unstable connections.
- Single job of this surface: make a relevant visitor quickly understand what Noveno does, who it is for, and take the primary action — **درخواست بررسی مسیر جذب** (request an acquisition-path review/audit).
- Desired user feeling before → after: scattered and unsure about why leads leak → **clear, calm, convinced this is a system, not a sales pitch**; the site itself must prove Noveno's own philosophy (Spec §83).
- Success signal: qualified audit submissions (see `docs/PRODUCT.md`).

## 2. Brand character

From Spec §45/§82/§83 — useful tensions, not vague adjectives:

- clear and systematic, not flashy or "AI-futuristic"
- calm and precise, not generic agency/SaaS
- measured and honest, not hyped or guaranteeing sales
- business-first and practical, not tech-first or tool-logo-heavy

## 3. Accepted visual thesis (founder override, 2026-08-14)

> **«تایپوگرافی + عکاسی + تصویر واقعی محصول + عددهای تحریریه»** — Noveno is presented as a well-run editorial publication about real service businesses: strong Persian typography carries the argument, real photography supplies the human context, real product screenshots supply the proof, and large editorial numerals carry every sequence. The site stays systematic **without drawing systems as diagrams**.

### 3.1 Founder decision — the flowchart language is rejected

The founder reviewed the rendered website twice and rejected the entire line-diagram family: Journey Line / route band / station-and-rail / node-and-connector / scatter diagrams / channel maps / miniature process graphs. The judgment is explicit:

- line diagrams feel technical and documentation-like;
- they look too small relative to the page;
- they create visual noise and weaken hierarchy;
- they do not feel premium and do not communicate quickly;
- they make the website feel sparse and abstract;
- they are visually repetitive.

**This is an override of the previous «مسیر»/Journey-Line thesis (§17, 2026-08-11 and 2026-08-14 first redesign).** Do not improve, resize, or iterate on those diagrams; the visual concept itself is rejected. Do not reintroduce a similar node-and-line grammar under another name. The word «مسیر» survives only as **product vocabulary** (the fixed CTA «درخواست بررسی مسیر جذب», offer names, and copy) — never as a drawn route.

### 3.2 The four visual primitives

1. **Strong editorial typography** — large Persian headlines, controlled supporting copy, deliberate asymmetric composition. Text itself carries the design: bold display type, strong numbers, clear hierarchy, confident whitespace. Empty space is composed, not filled with diagrams.
2. **Real product surfaces** — the main visual proof mechanism (homepage hero, system section, `/work`, `/work/[slug]`). Real work looks like real work: large restrained product previews of the audit interface and delivered sites. No screenshots inside fake decorative dashboards. Concept work uses **designed concept previews** (designed page mockups, never wireframe-diagrams) always labeled «نمونه نمایشی — سناریوی مفهومی».
3. **Restrained brand geometry** — hairline rules, square/circle primitives, subtle grid texture, logo-derived geometry. Used to frame product surfaces and to give the final CTA a memorable, quiet finish. Never decorative pattern walls.
4. **Editorial numbers and simple sequences** — where the old site drew process, the new site types it: «۰۱ جذب / ۰۲ متقاعدسازی / ۰۳ اقدام / …». Large numerals, strong headings, short descriptions, grid or vertical editorial layout. No connector line; sequence is communicated by ordering and typography.

Photography is **not** a primitive (third review, §3.5).

### 3.4 Media rhythm rule

Aim for **4–7 meaningful visual/media moments per long page** — never one per section, never decorative filler. Every image must improve understanding, credibility, emotional connection, proof, or composition. The homepage uses ≈6 moments: hero brand artwork, problem editorial typographic states, real audit-UI screenshot, large work previews, (process stays typographic), brand-led final CTA.

### 3.5 Third review — photography retired from the public visual language (2026-09)

**Decision: contextual business photography is no longer a Noveno visual primitive.** The hero photograph, the problem-section photograph, and the final-CTA photograph are removed; the homepage photography slots are replaced by:

1. **Real product/UI surfaces** — the audit interface and real site surfaces (this site's own build) are the primary substantial visual material;
2. **Typography-led editorial composition** — text and editorial numerals carry argumentative sections (problem, philosophy);
3. **Restrained Noveno brand geometry** — hairline rules, square/circle primitives, subtle grid texture, logo-derived geometry;
4. **Truthful work previews** — real project screenshots; concepts stay clearly labeled «نمونه نمایشی — سناریوی مفهومی».

Target ratio: `typography/layout + real product UI + restrained brand geometry`, not photography. The hero must visually reinforce `توجه ← درخواست ← ثبت ← پیگیری` using real Noveno interface material, remain clean enough that the headline stays dominant, and never become a fake dashboard, invented CRM UI, browser-frame cliché, or decorative SaaS card. The final CTA is a brand-led typographic finish (strong type + CTA + restrained surface + logo geometry), never a photo.

Photography assets and their processing scripts were removed from the production path (`docs/IMAGERY.md` records the retirement and retention decision). The `figure.media-frame` language survives for **real product screenshots and work previews only**.

### 3.6 Fourth review — the hero becomes brand artwork; Insights becomes وبلاگ (2026-10)

**Decision: the primary Hero visual is no longer a product screenshot.** The real audit-UI composition (third review) proved the product but did not answer the founder's core visual concern: the hero should communicate **brand + concept**, not show a product surface. The hero now carries an original Noveno graphical signature — «نشانه‌های پراکنده → سامانهٔ منظم» (Scattered Signal → Structured System):

- a field of small scattered marks (rotated squares, dots, ticks — incoming attention) that perceptibly settles into an ordered system: a hairline **system plate** of registered squares whose pattern converges on the **Noveno mark drawn as a quiet hairline attractor** (vertex squares locked onto its geometry);
- chaos → order is **perceived** through alignment, spacing and orientation — never drawn as arrows, connectors, or labels; no text inside the art;
- the transition reads RTL (chaos right, structure left) so the eye flows from the headline into the artwork;
- restrained palette: semantic tokens only (text/line/line-strong/primary on canvas) — the artwork re-themes itself with Light/Dark; one subtle `surface-soft` plate; faint 60px field grid; no glassmorphism, no glows;
- motion (transform/opacity only): a handful of transition marks settle into alignment once, the plate surface materializes, one registered square blinks an acknowledgment after load, and a ≤6px two-layer pointer parallax runs on fine-pointer desktop only; `prefers-reduced-motion` renders the fully settled frame;
- **static-first**: the artwork is one inline SVG (≈4 KB, no raster, no library, no remote asset); the hero's LCP is now the headline text — the 36 KB hero screenshot and its preload are gone;
- desktop: the square field hangs slightly below the text block and bleeds 32px out of its column; mobile: the artwork is **recomposed** as a wide strip (shelf band + registered row + settling marks + scatter) — fewer, larger marks, never a shrunk desktop SVG.

**Proof stays below the hero**: real product screenshots remain the proof mechanism in the system section, `/work`, and work detail pages.

### 3.7 Fifth review — focus over volume (founder direction, 2026-10)

**Decision: the site becomes fewer, shorter, more decisive pages.** The thesis of §3 (strong Persian typography + real product surfaces + restrained brand geometry + editorial numerals) is unchanged; what changes is **quantity of message**. Noveno stopped presenting itself as a broad agency with many loosely related services and now presents one specialist claim, one starting price, and one next step.

- **Homepage:** materially shorter — hero → the problem in a few lines → how the system works in **three** steps → real proof → three offers with تومان starting prices → short founder section → short FAQ → final CTA. Sections that repeated each other (a separate process section, a six-item principles grid, a fit list, a blog teaser band) were removed from the homepage; their substance survives on the page that owns it. **A section is not created just because it appears in a list.**
- **Copy discipline:** every section earns its place. Long hero paragraphs, SEO copy stuffed into headings, generated-sounding marketing lines, and decorative metrics are removed. Unverified numbers (lead counts, conversion rates, growth percentages, dashboard tiles) are deleted — there is no measured client data behind them.
- **Navigation:** reduced to **خانه · راهکارها · نمونه‌کار · قیمت · دربونه** plus the single primary action. `/services` and `/work` keep their URLs (only the nav labels changed to «راهکارها» / «نمونه‌کار»); `/pricing` is a new additive route; `/process`, `/blog`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms` stay reachable (footer and internal links) and keep their SEO ownership. Blog leaves primary navigation until it holds enough real writing to act as a credibility signal — no article is deleted and no URL breaks. **Superseded by the eighth review: `/blog` is back in the primary navigation as the fourth item** — «خانه · راهکارها · نمونه‌کار · وبلاگ · قیمت · درباره». The condition the fifth review set (enough real writing to act as a credibility signal) is met: six articles are published.
- **Offers:** three understandable tiers with «از …» starting prices in تومان, the middle one («سیستم جذب») visually recommended through a quiet typographic label only — never by fake scarcity, countdown, or strikethrough pricing. Recurring work is presented as productized monthly options; e-commerce is a secondary custom-project line, never a flagship.
- **Founder:** «دانیال رشیدی — بنیان‌گذار نوونو» appears concisely on the homepage and About. The layout reserves a portrait slot that is intentionally empty until a real photograph exists; no generated or stock face is ever substituted.
- **First contact:** the audit journey is one short screen (name, phone, business type, site-or-instagram, one problem field) instead of a six-step interview. Submission, storage, notification, attribution, abuse mitigation, and the never-false-success rule are unchanged.
- **Motion, founder direction (2026-10, verbatim intent):** «انیمیشن‌های باحال و جذاب اضافه بکن، بدون اینکه از پرفورمنس کم بشه» — the site should _feel_ alive: motion is now an explicit product quality, not just leftover polish. The hard constraint is the second half of the sentence: **no measurable performance cost** — no extra JavaScript, no paint-bound animation, no new bytes in the critical path, and no regression in LCP/CLS/INP. Read together with the earlier brand decisions, «باحال» means _precise and product-specific_, not neon, bouncy, or template-generic: the animation must be the site's own idea (signals registering into a system), not a downloaded effect. Palette (§6), type (§5), composition (§8), media (§9) and the reduced-motion contract (§12) are unchanged by this direction.
- **Media rhythm:** with fewer sections, the homepage carries ≈4 visual moments (hero brand artwork, real audit-UI figure, two real work previews, brand-led final CTA). No filler media is added to fill removed sections.

**Insights → Blog:** the section previously called «دیدگاه‌ها» (/insights) is now a conventional professional **وبلاگ** at `/blog` and `/blog/[slug]`. Old `/insights` URLs permanently redirect (301) to `/blog` (`public/_redirects`) — one canonical article URL. The publishing workflow stays Markdown-first (`docs/BLOG.md`).

### 3.8 Homepage blog section (eighth review, 2026-10)

**Owner decision:** the blog is in the header and has one section on the homepage. **Accepted composition** (agent proposal under that decision, reviewed as rendering evidence):

- **Position in the sequence:** directly after the proof section and before the offers — *what we do → what we have built → what we write about it → what it costs*. The decision run-up (offers → segments → founder → FAQ → final CTA) is untouched, and the first viewport is untouched.
- **Reading: a publication's index, not a card wall.** The section is typographic end to end: one **lead entry** (the newest published article) with its reference, description and a single «خواندن نوشته» affordance, followed by hairline **ledger rows** (numeral ۰۲، ۰۳، ۰۴ — reference plus title only), and one closing action to `/blog`. Every row is **one anchor wrapping the whole row**, so the target is the row and an article costs exactly one tab stop; the affordance inside it is therefore `ArrowLink as="span"` — the same glyph and the same hover rules, rendered non-interactively, so no link is nested and no glyph is duplicated. No thumbnails, no cards, no shadows, no connector lines, no badges beyond the existing plain category tag — the same discipline as `/blog` (`docs/BLOG.md`: «No thumbnails required»).
- **Structure is a running head:** the entry text at the inline start (right, RTL), its reference — category, date, reading time — in a column at the inline end (`sm:w-48 xl:w-56`, narrowed at `sm` so the 640–900 px band keeps a readable text column). Below `sm` the reference stacks above the title, so the row is recomposed rather than compressed.
- **Signature carry-over:** the `.editorial-num` device (§4.2) and the hairline rule do the work; each row's rule draws in from the RTL start and its numeral locks once as the section registers (§12 registration layer, devices 1 and 2) — **zero new JavaScript**. The interactive half of the signature is the **row's own hairline registering in `primary` on hover *and* keyboard focus** (§12 device 5c, the same treatment a work preview gets), together with the title's colour change; it replaces a margin-square marker that was built, reviewed in pixels, and dropped because at this column width it detached from its row and read as a stray glyph.
- **The lead is promoted by weight, not by a new type step:** `text-h3` at weight 700 with `text-wrap: balance`, plus the description and the affordance. `text-h2` is deliberately **not** used — at 1440 it is the same 40 px as the section's own heading, and an article title must stay subordinate to it. The ledger rows stay at `text-h3` 600.
- **Tokens:** `text-action` numerals, `line` rules, `text-faint` meta, `primary` registration line, `surface` category tag — no new color, no new type step, no new spacing value.
- **Truth:** the section renders the real `blog` collection through the existing `isPublished` gate, newest first (the same order as `/blog`). Drafts never appear. Nothing is counted, rated or promised; the count line states the number of published articles only.
- **Empty state:** with zero published articles the section keeps its header and states the honest position («هنوز نوشته‌ای منتشر نشده است») with one link to `/blog`. It never renders filler.

## 4. Signature visual language

**Signature: the editorial media figure + the large numeral + the signal field.** Three restrained devices repeat across the site:

1. **Media figure** — `figure.media-figure` → `div.media-frame` (hairline border, 4px radius, reserved aspect ratio: hero 3:2, product 16:10, work 16:10) + `figcaption.media-caption` (12–13px Estedad 500, `text-faint`, states what the real surface is). All real product screenshots and work previews use this frame; it is the site's consistent picture language.
2. **Editorial numeral** — `.editorial-num`: Estedad 800, `clamp(2rem, 1.5rem + 2.2vw, 3.25rem)` (32→52px), `text-action` ink, tabular. Used for: system stages (۰۱–۰۶), process steps (۰۱–۰۵), offers (۰۱–۰۳), why-Noveno rows, 404 («۴۰۴»). Numbers are content, never decoration.
3. **Signal field** (hero + OG card) — the scatter-to-structure brand artwork (§3.6): a field of marks converging on the Noveno mark as attractor. This is the hero's visual identity and the homepage social card's geometry.

**What the site never draws anymore:** connected lines, stations, nodes on rails, scatter fields, channel maps, mini process graphs, route motifs, dotted route extensions, square/circle connector systems, or any decorative system-architecture diagram.

## 5. Typography — Persian as a first-class brand decision

### 5.1 Decision, source, license (unchanged — accepted 2026-08-11)

| Role                | Family                  | Source                                      | License         | Weight axis            |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ---------------------- |
| Display / UI / data | **Estedad** (استعداد)   | npm `@fontsource-variable/estedad` v5.3.0   | **SIL OFL 1.1** | wght 100–900, variable |
| Body                | **Vazirmatn** (وزیرمتن) | npm `@fontsource-variable/vazirmatn` v5.3.0 | **SIL OFL 1.1** | wght 100–900, variable |

Rationale: Estedad display — designed for screen use, compact, geometric, less ubiquitous than Vazirmatn, so headings read as deliberate; Vazirmatn body — the most battle-tested Persian text face. Both include Latin, both render Persian digits ۰–۹, both variable (one file per subset). Self-hosted under `public/fonts/`; no external font CDN.

### 5.2 Type roles and scale

Base: 16px; scale in `rem`. Line-heights generous because Persian joins four levels.

| Role               | Family    | Size / line-height                                              | Weight  | Use                                   |
| ------------------ | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Display (hero)     | Estedad   | `clamp(2.5rem, 1.4rem + 4vw, 4rem)` (40→64px) · lh 1.24         | 800     | Homepage hero only                    |
| Page H1            | Estedad   | `clamp(1.875rem, 1.35rem + 2.2vw, 3rem)` (30→48px) · lh 1.3     | 800     | All non-home routes                   |
| H2 (section)       | Estedad   | `clamp(1.5rem, 1.15rem + 1.8vw, 2.5rem)` (24→40px) · lh 1.35    | 700     | Section titles                        |
| H3                 | Estedad   | `clamp(1.125rem, 1.05rem + 0.4vw, 1.375rem)` (18→22px) · lh 1.6 | 600     | Sub-blocks, offer names               |
| Editorial numeral  | Estedad   | `clamp(2rem, 1.5rem + 2.2vw, 3.25rem)` (32→52px) · lh 1         | 800     | Sequences (۰۱…), 404                  |

**No letter-spacing on Persian text** (breaks joining); tracking 0 for display; the Latin «NOVENO» wordmark may use modest +0.02–0.04em tracking.

### 5.3 Fallback strategy (CLS-safe, unchanged)

Stack: `"Estedad", "Estedad Fallback", "Vazirmatn", "Noto Sans Arabic", "Segoe UI", Tahoma, sans-serif`; body: `"Vazirmatn", "Vazirmatn Fallback", …`. `font-display: swap`; **preload only `estedad-arabic`** (the hero headline is the only LCP element — the hero carries no raster media since the fourth review). Metric-matched Tahoma fallback faces declared locally (CLS target ≤ 0.1).

### 5.4 Performance (measured, unchanged)

Four variable woff2 subsets ≈ **165 KB total**; budget **≤ 200 KB**. Served from `public/fonts/` with `Cache-Control: immutable` via Cloudflare Pages.

## 12. Motion — the small reusable system (5 behaviors, site-wide)

Restrained; clarifies state or hierarchy only. The whole site uses **five reusable behaviors** (one shared `IntersectionObserver` in `src/scripts/motion.ts`; no animation library):

1. **Section reveal** — below-the-fold groups only: opacity + 12px translate, 450ms, stagger 45ms where a sequence matters. Content is visible by default (no-JS, no-CLS, no-LCP risk); hero content never animates.
2. **Hero moment** — the hero artwork's quiet settle: a handful of signals find alignment (one-shot, staggered), the plate surface materializes, one registered square blinks an acknowledgment; the stage strip under the artwork fades in with a tiny translate, and the accent line under the kicker grows (transform-only). LCP-critical content (headline) is painted immediately and never moves; the artwork's natural (settled) state is the default frame — no-JS and reduced-motion see it instantly. Pointer parallax (≤6px, two layers) only on fine-pointer desktop (`motion.ts initHeroParallax`).
3. **Link/button micro** — directional icon movement on hover/focus (work rows, contact rows), nav underline grows from the RTL start, pressed scale 0.98 on buttons (150ms).
4. **Work previews** — very small scale (1.015) on hover **and** keyboard focus (`:focus-within`), pointer devices only via hover media where practical.
5. **Mobile menu** — opacity + 6px translate open/close (250ms), `hidden` applied after the close transition, focus moved in/out, Escape closes.

Rules: transform/opacity only; no blur, no filters, no continuous loops, no raw-scroll listeners, no scroll-jacking, no parallax, no per-section reveals of every paragraph. Reduced motion: `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` zeroes all durations in CSS AND `motion.ts` skips adding hidden starting states, so everything renders directly in its final state.

### Registration layer (added 2026-10 — founder motion direction, §0)

The site's own idea — _scattered signals registering into a structured system_ — is now also its motion signature. It is an **extension of the five behaviors above, not a sixth system**: it reuses `motion.ts`'s single `IntersectionObserver` and the existing `.reveal-init` → `.reveal-in` class swap, and it ships **zero additional JavaScript**. Six devices:

1. **Rule draw (`data-sweep`)** — the hairline that opens a section or a sequence row is _drawn_ from the inline start (right, in RTL) when its group registers: a canvas-colored cover strip retracts to the start edge (`scaleX` 1 → 0, transform-only, 520ms, 90ms after the group lands). The rule itself never moves, is never re-colored, and is fully present with no JS, with reduced motion, and for any group the observer never touches. `[data-sweep="bottom"]` is the same device for `SectionHeader`'s rule, so the section opener reads the same way on every route.
2. **Editorial numeral lock** — `.editorial-num` inside a registering group draws a 2px `primary` bar under itself (420ms) and lets it fade out (260ms, delayed 520ms). The settled frame is unchanged: a flash of registration, never a permanent underline.
3. **Frame resolve** — a `media-frame`/`preview-stage` image inside a registering group settles from `scale(1.02)`, so real proof surfaces arrive with the geometry instead of sliding as a flat block.
4. **Hero registration sweep** — after the existing settle, one hairline scan with a small `primary` lead square crosses the hero plate once (right → left, 900ms, 1150ms), and the acknowledgment blink follows it. One shot, transform/opacity, no loop. The mobile strip gets its own recomposed sweep.
5. **Interactive registration (`hover: hover` / `:focus-within`)** — a work/concept preview draws a 1px `primary` hairline across its frame edge on hover _and_ keyboard focus; a blog index row registers the same way on its own hairline (§3.8, device 5c); `ArrowLink` grows its underline from the inline start and nudges its glyph 4px; the FAQ answer registers (6px rise + fade) as its panel opens. Touch devices never see a stuck state.
6. **Registration line on the mobile sticky header** — a 2px `primary` line fills with scroll position through a CSS scroll-driven animation (`animation-timeline: scroll(root block)`), no scroll listener and no JS. It exists only where the header is sticky (mobile; the desktop header is `static` per §8) and only inside `@supports` + `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, so older engines simply show the plain hairline.

Performance rules for this layer (the reason it stays free): transform/opacity only — never `filter`, `box-shadow`, `backdrop-filter`, `width/height/top/left`; no `will-change` left behind; every animation is one-shot with `both` fill; no JS is added; the settled state is the natural CSS state, so nothing depends on an animation completing.

### 16.1 Seventh review — execution corrections (2026-10)

Agent-labelled execution work under the owner decision above. **The concept is unchanged**: «نشانه‌های پراکنده → سامانهٔ منظم» is still the hero's visual idea, still one inline SVG, still transform/opacity-only motion, still semantic tokens only. What changed is how faithfully the rendering expresses it, plus three non-visual corrections. Each item cites the rendered evidence that produced it.

1. **The attractor must read as the attractor.** Measured at 1440×900 (settled frame), the Noveno mark inside the plate rendered at an effective ~0.96 px stroke in `line-strong` at 0.62 opacity — the faintest element in the frame, so the piece read as "some dots and a small grid". The mark is now drawn in `text-muted` at a real hairline weight so it is the strongest hairline on the plate, with its four green vertex squares locked on. Still quiet, still hairline, no glow and no fill.
2. **Structure must dominate scatter in the settled frame.** The plate previously occupied 280×280 of a 600×600 viewBox (≈22 %, corner-anchored), leaving a dead upper-left quadrant; the founder's earlier rejection of the route language included "looks too small relative to the page". The plate is now the compositional anchor of the frame at a larger share, with the scatter field reading as the incoming side. The fade mask, settle marks, sweep, and acknowledgment blink are unchanged in behaviour.
3. **The mobile recomposition must not clip the mark.** In the 390 px strip the mark was translated to `x=10` while the shelf band starts at `x=24`, so the attractor hung outside its own system and was sliced by the frame edge; at `scale(0.12` it was also ~30 px wide on screen. The mark is now scaled and placed inside the shelf, and the shelf's registered row reads at 123 px strip height.
4. **FAQ answers are content, not decoration.** `FAQItem`'s collapsed state was the CSS default, so all 15 answers on `/` and `/services` were `24 px / opacity 0` whenever JavaScript did not run — including blocked scripts, a realistic condition for this audience (`docs/PRODUCT.md`). The collapsed state is now applied only after the component script initializes, matching the pattern `[data-reveal]` already uses. Visible by default; no flash of open panels on load.
5. **`primary-soft` needs its own ink.** The `/work` selected-filter chip paired `text-action` (#2f7a5e) on `primary-soft` (#e3efe9) = **4.37:1**, below the 4.5:1 requirement — a token-pairing error of exactly the kind §6.2 warns about, since `text-action` is verified against `canvas`, not against the selected-chip surface. The chip now uses the existing `--on-primary-soft` pair (light #0c3d2b on #e3efe9 = 10.35:1; dark #e8f3ed on #173026 ≈ 12:1). No token value changed; the correct existing pair is used.
6. **One price surface, one owner.** `/`, `/services` and `/pricing` each rendered the full `OfferRow` block, while `/services`' own meta description says prices live on `/pricing`. `/services` no longer re-renders the offers; the homepage keeps the founder-accepted starting prices (DESIGN §3.7) but in compact rows — numeral, name, starting price, framing, link — with scope points and the price note living on `/pricing`, which stays the canonical price page. No price changed, no URL removed.
7. **Interior page covers.** `PageHero` renders a short text column inside a 511 px band, leaving the entire left half of a 1440 px first viewport empty. The cover rhythm is tightened so the whitespace reads as composed rather than unfinished. No second column, no new decoration, and the homepage hero is untouched.
8. **A case study must not link to a route that does not exist.** The «منابع» list on all five real `/work/[slug]` case studies ended with a self-referential item, «صفحهٔ نمونه‌کار نوونو», pointing at `https://noveno.ir/portfolio/` — a page that is not part of the section grammar (`/work` is the portfolio) and was never built, so it resolved to the 404 page for every reader and crawler. The item is removed rather than repointed: a case study citing its own listing page is circular inside a list whose job is verifiable external sources. The list keeps its real external citations, its `<ul>` semantics, its Persian names, and its existing type/colour tokens — verified unchanged at 7.20:1 (light) and 9.60:1 (dark). `tests/seo-contract.test.mjs` now resolves every same-origin `href`/`src` in the built HTML against the contents of `dist/`, so this class of defect fails the gate instead of shipping silently.
